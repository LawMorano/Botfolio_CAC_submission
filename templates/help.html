How Botfolio works

Everything you need to get a design online, plus the right inbox for whatever you need a hand with.

  1. Upload your design

    Upload your CAD file and view it directly in your browser. Rotate, zoom, and inspect the model before publishing.

  2. Choose your best view

    Position the model to highlight the mechanism, then capture the preview that will represent your design throughout Botfolio.

  3. Share, tag & improve

    Publish your design so other teams can discover it, suggest tags, and learn from it. Request AI-assisted feedback when you want another perspective.
